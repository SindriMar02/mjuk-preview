# MJÚK Iceland

**Register:** brand (image-led shop; design is a large part of what is being sold)

**What it is.** The restructured storefront for mjukiceland.com. WooCommerce stays the engine
underneath; this is the face. Knitwear designed, produced and sold by one family company in
Reykjavík: their own factory, yarn colours mixed only for them, about fifty thousand hats a
year, four shops downtown, a workshop above the flagship.

**Who it is for.** Mostly American tourists and mail-order customers who have been in one of the
shops, plus Icelanders who buy the Tenderness hats. They shop by colour first, then by fibre.
Many arrive from an Instagram tag. They are not browsing; they know they want a hat and they want
to see it in twenty colours.

**Brand voice, three words.** Handmade, exact, unbothered. (Anna: "It's not perfect, but it's
functional." The site should feel like her workshop, not like a fashion house.)

**Locked identity.** Porcelain ground `#f7f3ef`, ink `#17121a`, ember accent `#a8320a`.
Bodoni Moda for serif moments, Humane for the display word, Space Mono for labels. These were
chosen before this project and are shipping; identity-preservation wins over the reflex-reject
lists. Product photography is full colour always; only atmosphere imagery may be desaturated.

**The two things the site must do that the old one does not.**
1. Show the pompom option on the hat itself: none, one or two, colours as photographs, price
   updating, and a single prompt when a plain hat goes in the bag.
2. Take a made-to-order poncho or cape without an email chain: shape, then fabric (one of her
   blankets), then salmon leather trim, one final price.

**Constraints.** Stock is a deliberate buffer (three of everything, one reserve piece), so never
display counts. Sale must stay visible. English first, Icelandic as a real second version at
launch. Card grid and rails are already tuned for 390px and must not be broken.
