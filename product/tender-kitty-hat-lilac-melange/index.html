Description
Playful, smooth, intentionally understated A soft, architectural M silhouette reminds a kitty shape in a subtle way. This loose, relaxed fit hat gives you space for experiments with styling- wearing it in full length or rolling it up. In any case the hat will be cozy and comfortable, since it won’t create any pressure on your head. Soothing, calming and cute- all this is about this design. Fun shape with subtle ears silhouette Super smooth angora merino wool blend Medium level of warmth Relaxed fit Care & Material Like all MJÚK products, these hats are made to last. To keep your Kitty Hat soft and smooth, we recommend dry cleaning or gentle hand washing in lukewarm water and laying flat to dry. Please, handle the hat gently- it is a soft material in a delicate knit. If you get it wet under rain or snow- no worries. Just put it flat on a towel and let it dry slowly. Composition: 50% angora for warmth and light fluffiness, 15% viscose for silky feeling , 10% wool for even better insulation and 25% polyacrylic to make it durable
