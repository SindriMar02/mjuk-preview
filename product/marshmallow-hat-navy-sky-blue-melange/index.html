Description

The Design

The Marshmallow collection was created to make a bold, joyful statement against the winter chill. With its exceptionally fluffy texture and fun, feminine aesthetic, this piece is designed to stand out. The name perfectly captures its essence: a cozy, voluminous accessory that feels like a cloud. To bring this playful vision to life, we developed a series of vibrant, exclusive yarn shades that are entirely unique to this specific line.

The Design

Despite its striking, big-volume look, the hat feels completely weightless when worn. We designed this beanie with super flexibility. This zero-pressure approach, combined with a breathable unlined construction, delivers effortless, all-day comfort.

Your Style

Because personal style is all about choice, we offer this striking design in an extensive pallete of 35 colors that we constantly refresh. You can easily build a cohesive, vibrant winter set by pairing your beanie with our matching Marshmallow gloves and mittens, all crafted from the exact same fluffy yarn and our signature Alpaca silk Lía scarves, which special yarn was dyed to match the Marshmallow collection.

MJÚK Custom experience

Because true exclusivity lies in personal choice, we invite you to make this piece entirely your own. Choose a special pompom for your beanie online or visit our in-boutique pompom station to customize your beanie on the spot, selecting from a collection of over 80 specially dyed colors to create your perfect beanie.

The Mjúk Promise

In Icelandic, Mjúk means soft—a guiding principle for both our materials and our respect for the environment. As the celebrated Icelandic Company of the Year, we produce in strictly limited quantities to minimize waste. To extend the life of your garment, we provide dedicated repair services directly from our flagship workshop.

Care & Composition

Composition: Fluffy Angora Wool Blend. 60% Angora, 40% PA

Maintenance: Handwash only in cold water (20°C). Lay flat to dry; never twist or wring the delicate fibers.

Note: Due to the artisanal dyeing process and natural fibers, slight color variations may occur, ensuring your piece is beautifully unique.
