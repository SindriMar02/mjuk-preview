Description
Crafted from fluffy angora for a delightfully soft finish Roots beanie features a chunky ribbed-knit that hosts a special tribute to Icelandic cultural roots. Hence the name. We combined trendy shape of the beanie and trident pattern from the Vegvisir. Literally “way” (vegr) “the one that shows” (vísir), the symbol is meant to prevent the person carrying it from losing their way , and is known colloquially as “The Viking Compass” or “The Nordic Compass”. Most of the yarn colors were specially developed for this collection and you can find them only in this line. Feel special- choose your unique color of the hat. Fluffy and soft angora wool blend.
