## Full
### The Story
True luxury is born from personal devotion. This profoundly soft, limited-edition beanie is crafted from an innovative, hypoallergenic blend originally developed by Anna for her baby daughter, Lía. Hand-finished in our Reykjavík atelier at Laugavegur 23—the historic house with the Valkyrie mural—each piece reflects a commitment to sustainable, ethical craftsmanship that has defined our family’s manufacturing legacy since 1991.

### The Sensation
Experience weightless warmth. Knitted from an exclusive blend of 88% Baby Suri Alpaca and 12% Silk, this garment offers exceptional breathability and natural temperature regulation. The unique composition ensures it remains gentle and soft, providing a soothing touch even for the most sensitive skin. We produce 2 versions of Lía beanies: bouclé and brushed. In bouclé yarn silk is wrapping alpaca wool and you feel a more silky smooth texture. In the brushed version the hat is fluffier and you feel alpaca wool more than silk.

The term "bouclé" comes from the French word meaning "curled," which describes the yarns curls of wool and silk- its loops and knots made on purpose during spinning. In the fashion world, bouclé has held a steady place for decades, particularly in luxurious clothing pieces.

### MJÚK Iceland Promise
In Icelandic, Mjúk means soft—a philosophy that extends to how we treat our environment. As the celebrated Icelandic Company of the Year, we produce in strictly limited quantities to minimize waste. To ensure your garment lasts a lifetime, we offer dedicated repair and customization services directly from our flagship workshop.

### Care & Composition
Exclusive yarn: specially spun 88% Baby Suri Alpaca, 12% Silk.

Maintenance: Dry clean or gently handwash in cold water (20°C). Lay flat to dry; never twist or wring the delicate fibers.

Note: Due to the artisanal dyeing process and natural fibers, slight color variations may occur, ensuring your piece is beautifully unique.
