## Full
### The Design
The Chalet collection is our definitive answer to deep winter. While crafted from the same exceptionally fluffy angora wool blend as our Marshmallow line, the Chalet is built with a substantially thicker knit for superior insulation. Its striking, bold silhouette offers a larger, more generous fit that sits deeply and comfortably on the head. To bring this vision to life, we utilize our exclusive series of vibrant, custom yarn shades.

### The Fit
Uncompromising warmth meets zero-pressure comfort. As one of the warmest hats we produce, the Chalet beanie is fortified with a full interior lining that acts as a complete windproof barrier against the harshest chills. Despite its substantial construction and impressive volume, the hat maintains super flexibility. It is specifically tailored to embrace the head gently, delivering effortless, all-day comfort without feeling tight or restrictive.

### Your Style
Because personal style is all about choice, we offer this striking design in an extensive palette of 35 colors that we constantly refresh. You can easily build a cohesive, vibrant winter set by pairing your beanie with our matching Marshmallow gloves and mittens, all crafted from the exact same fluffy yarn and our signature Alpaca silk Lía scarves, which special yarn was dyed to match the Marshmallow collection.

### MJÚK Custom experience
Because true exclusivity lies in personal choice, we invite you to make this piece entirely your own. Choose a special pompom for your beanie online or visit our in-boutique pompom station to customize your beanie on the spot, selecting from a collection of over 80 specially dyed colors to create your perfect beanie.

### The Mjúk Promise
In Icelandic, Mjúk means soft—a guiding principle for both our materials and our respect for the environment. As the celebrated Icelandic Company of the Year, we produce in strictly limited quantities to minimize waste. To extend the life of your garment, we provide dedicated repair services directly from our flagship workshop.

### Care & Composition
Composition: Fluffy Angora Wool Blend, fleece lining. 60% Angora, 40% PA

Maintenance: Handwash only in cold water (20°C). Lay flat to dry; never twist or wring the delicate fibers.

Note: Due to the artisanal dyeing process and natural fibers, slight color variations may occur, ensuring your piece is beautifully unique.
