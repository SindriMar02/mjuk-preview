## Full
### The Story
While cashmere is traditionally crafted in thin layers due to its inherent warmth, we engineered this piece as a substantially thicker alternative to combat the harsh realities of the Icelandic winter. Hand-finished in our Reykjavík atelier, this scarf provides a level of protection significantly warmer than typical cashmere accessories available globally. Built for enduring, reliable performance, it has been rigorously tested in extreme sub-zero climates spanning Iceland, Greenland, Norway, and Northern Canada.

### The Design
By using a generous amount of certified, ethically sourced pure cashmere, we created a voluminous rib knit that provides complete wind protection. This specific rib knit gives the scarf exceptional structural integrity, allowing it to hold a beautiful, architectural drape over the shoulders. Measuring 30x186 cm (14" x 87"), its compact yet voluminous size provide versatile styling options without feeling restrictive or overloading. Because it is crafted from natural fibers, it remains exceptionally breathable, delivering profound warmth alongside continuous ventilation.

In Icelandic, Mjúk means soft—a philosophy that extends to how we treat our environment. Operating our own local manufacturing alongside our retail boutique, and honored as the Icelandic Company of the Year in 2024 and 2025, we produce in strictly limited quantities to minimize waste. ### Care & Composition
Dimensions: 30x186 cm (14" x 87").

Composition: 98% Ethically Sourced Cashmere, 2% Elastane to hold the scarf’s structure.

Maintenance: Dry clean or gently handwash in cold water (20°C). Lay flat to dry; never twist or wring the delicate fibers.

Note: Due to the artisanal dyeing process and natural fibers, slight color variations may occur, ensuring your piece is beautifully unique.
