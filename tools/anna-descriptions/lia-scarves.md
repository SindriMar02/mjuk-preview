## Full
### The Story
True luxury is a labor of devotion. This limited-edition scarf represents over a year of research alongside our masterful yarn suppliers. Crafted from an innovative material developed by Anna specifically for her baby daughter, Lía, it reflects the commitment to sustainable, ethical craftsmanship. Hand-finished in our Reykjavík atelier at Laugavegur 23—the historic house with the Valkyrie mural—each piece reflects a commitment to sustainable, ethical craftsmanship that has defined our family’s manufacturing legacy since 1991. Designed to pair flawlessly with our signature beanies, this piece is a testament to uncompromising quality.

### The Sensation
Experience true comfort without the bulk. Tailored to the dimensions of 35 x 245 cm (14" x 96"), the scarf provides generous length for versatile styling and a refined, elegant drape. Woven from 88% Baby Suri Alpaca and 12% Silk, these naturally thermoregulating fibers adapt instantly to your environment. The unique hypoallergenic composition ensures a soothing touch, offering unparalleled comfort even for the most sensitive skin.

### The MJÚK Iceland Promise
In Icelandic, Mjúk means soft—a philosophy that extends to how we treat our environment. As the celebrated Icelandic Company of the Year, we produce in strictly limited quantities to minimize waste. To ensure your garment lasts a lifetime, we offer dedicated repair and customization services directly from our flagship workshop.

### Care & Composition
Dimensions: 35 x 245 cm (14" x 96").

Exclusive Yarn: specially spun 88% Baby Suri Alpaca, 12% Silk.

Maintenance: Dry clean or gently handwash in lukewarm water. Lay flat to air dry; never twist or wring the delicate fibers.

Note: Due to the artisanal dyeing process and natural fibers, slight color variations may occur, ensuring your piece is beautifully unique.
