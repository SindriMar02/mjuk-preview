## Full
### The Story
Cashmere is traditionally crafted in thin layers due to its inherent warmth. Viking beanie is made for effortless everyday elegance. It is an ideal companion for mild winters and transitional seasons. Hand-finished in our Reykjavík atelier by Mjúk Iceland, it delivers enduring, reliable luxury in a sleek, unisex Icelandic style.

### The Design
The design features a moderately flexible, gentle fit tailored to rest comfortably on the head without feeling restrictive. Because it is crafted entirely without a synthetic lining, the pure cashmere fibers remain remarkably breathable, delivering soothing warmth alongside continuous, well-ventilated comfort.

### MJÚK Custom experience
Because true exclusivity lies in personal choice, we invite you to make this piece entirely your own. Choose a special pompom for your beanie online or visit our in-boutique pompom station to customize your beanie on the spot, selecting from a collection of over 80 specially dyed colors to create your perfect beanie. This piece pairs flawlessly with our cashmere scarves, shawls, ponchos and gloves knitted from the exact same naturally soft yarn.

### MJÚK Iceland Promise
In Icelandic, Mjúk means soft—a philosophy that extends to how we treat our environment. As the celebrated Icelandic Company of the Year, we produce in strictly limited quantities to minimize waste. To ensure your garment lasts a lifetime, we offer dedicated repair and customization services directly from our flagship workshop.

### Care & Composition
Composition: 98% Ethically Sourced Cashmere, 2% Elastane to hold the hat's structure.

Maintenance: Dry clean or gently handwash in cold water (20°C). Lay flat to dry; never twist or wring the delicate fibers.

Note: Due to the artisanal dyeing process and natural fibers, slight color variations may occur, ensuring your piece is beautifully unique.
