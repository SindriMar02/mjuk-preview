## Full
### The Story
While cashmere is traditionally crafted in thin layers due to its inherent warmth, we engineered this piece as a substantially thicker alternative to combat the harsh realities of the Icelandic winter. Hand-finished in our Reykjavík atelier, this unisex piece provides a level of protection significantly warmer than typical cashmere hats available globally. Built for enduring, reliable performance, it has been rigorously tested in extreme sub-zero climates spanning Iceland, Greenland, Norway, and Northern Canada.

### The Design
By using a generous amount of certified, ethically sourced pure cashmere, we created a dense knit that provides complete wind protection. Despite its thickness, the design features a spacious, flexible silhouette tailored to rest lightly on the head, avoiding the tight fit of conventional winter hats. Because it is crafted without a synthetic lining, the natural cashmere fibers remain exceptionally breathable, delivering profound warmth alongside continuous ventilation.

### MJÚK Iceland Promise
In Icelandic, Mjúk means soft—a philosophy that extends to how we treat our environment. As the celebrated Icelandic Company of the Year, we produce in strictly limited quantities to minimize waste. To ensure your garment lasts a lifetime, we offer dedicated repair and customization services directly from our flagship workshop.

### Care & Composition
Composition: 98% Ethically Sourced Cashmere, 2% Elastane to hold the hat's structure.

Maintenance: Dry clean or gently handwash in cold water (20°C). Lay flat to dry; never twist or wring the delicate fibers.

Note: Due to the artisanal dyeing process and natural fibers, slight color variations may occur, ensuring your piece is beautifully unique.
