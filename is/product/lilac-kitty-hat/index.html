Classical nautical model of a hat. It is warm, flexible and its one size fits all. Unisex style. The hat feels comfortable, soft and won’t leave marks on the forehead. A light garment, which looks refined and still gives warmth and comfort.

30% cashmere, 70% superfine merino wool . The raw material comes from a factory established in 1850. The yarn doesnt include  azo dyes, formaldehyde, carcinogenic, hazardous or allergy inducing products. So it is not only a warm and soft beanie, but also a more sustainable and healthy product.

Care: dry cleaning or Handwash with cold water, gentle wool detergent, no twisting, airdry flat
